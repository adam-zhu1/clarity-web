    Press

Words in 8 seconds. Video in a minute.

What it's built with

On your Mac, and in the cloud.

On your Mac

  • Pythonthe menu-bar app
  • Gocoordinates each request
  • FastAPIthe agent service
  • LangGraphthe agents
  • Dockersandboxed rendering
  • MManimdraws the animation
  • ffmpegjoins the scenes

In the cloud

  • Geminireads and explains
  • Claudewrites the animation code
  • VVoyage AIfinds similar examples
  • MongoDB Atlasjobs, cache, examples
  • S3finished videos

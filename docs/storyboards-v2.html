Two ways to open with the product, not the logo

Both start from the same fact learned from the App Style winners: show the app doing its job in the first frame, on a light ground, with the download button visible immediately. A is a six-second film that ends on the page. B is the page, with the film looping quietly beside it. Same theme, same laptop, same windows rebuilt from the app's own CSS.

Trade-offs

A · the film

Stronger first impression, and it's your idea. It costs six seconds on every load, which is far above what the award sites in this category do; they show the product instantly. It works because the button is visible throughout and a click skips it. Best if you want the site to feel like a launch trailer.

  • Build effort: high (scene choreography + zoom + focus)
  • Risk: some visitors will click through before it ends

B · the live poster

This is what Cooldock, Supaste and Invoko actually do. Product and button in the first frame, motion that never asks for attention. Less theatrical, more confident. The laptop sliding across on the tab switch gives you a real transition moment without gating anything.

  • Build effort: medium (loop + slide)
  • Risk: less memorable on first load

My recommendation: build B first. Its laptop loop, windows and theme are exactly the pieces A needs. If B feels too quiet once it's real, A is B plus a six-second opening on top, not a rebuild.
